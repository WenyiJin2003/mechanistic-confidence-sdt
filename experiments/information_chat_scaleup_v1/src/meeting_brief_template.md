# Confidence Representations and the Next Step Toward SDT

## 开场先记住这三句话

**总目标：**研究模型内部是否有与 confidence 相关的表示，未来能否利用它，让模型通过 Synthetic Document Training（SDT，合成文档训练）更好地学会目标内容。

**这次完成的事：**固定 5,000 组信息型问答，每组保留原回答，并提供坚定、犹豫两个改写。在不更新大模型的前提下，用 4,000 组训练小 probe，1,000 组做测试，比较第 14 层和全部 28 层。

**结果边界：**现在测的是“能否识别回答表达得更自信”，不是回答正确率，也不是 SDT 已经有效。{{RESULT_INTERPRETATION}}

## 0 to 1 minute  Research question

My project asks whether we can identify a confidence-related representation
inside a language model, and eventually use it to improve how the model learns
target content.

The longer-term setting is Synthetic Document Training, or SDT. We train on
synthetic documents that contain the information we want the model to learn.
The idea we are exploring is to keep that ordinary training objective and add
an internal loss that encourages a particular confidence-related state.

The important distinction is that our goal is not just to make the model sound
more certain. We want it to learn and retain the target information better.
Before testing that training idea, I have been working on the measurement:
what exactly would this internal signal measure, and how reliably can we read
it out?

中文提示：先讲最终要解决的学习问题，不要以“我跑了几个 probe”开场。

## 1 to 2.5 minutes  How the earlier experiments shaped the question

I started with a small semantic-entropy probing experiment. It asked whether
the model's hidden states could predict how much its possible answers would
disagree. Initially, I extracted the state at the end of the question, before
the answer. I also tried a separate post-answer diagnostic.

That helped establish the pipeline, but disagreement among possible answers
is not the same as confidence expressed in a particular answer. So I moved to
paired responses with the same substantive content, written in confident and
hesitant language.

In a small independent challenge, a frozen layer-14 readout ranked the
confident member higher in 47 of 50 pairs. An ensemble across all 28 layers
ranked 48 of 50. Text-only and length controls were weaker on that challenge.
Those results motivated a larger test, but 50 pairs were not enough to settle
how general the signal was.

I also ran separate evidence-support diagnostics. They showed why I should
not equate this wording-related signal with correctness or support for an
answer. Those remain distinct questions rather than the labels for the
current experiment.

中文提示：之前的贡献是跑通方法、区分测量目标、得到小规模迁移结果。旧 50 对和新 5,000 组不是相同设置下的 learning curve。

## 2.5 to 4 minutes  Data and comparison

Following Cas's suggestion, I selected information-seeking conversations from
UltraChat and used Claude Haiku to produce two versions of each answer: one
confident and one hesitant. The instruction is to preserve the substantive
content while changing the degree of commitment, without adding a detached
sentence such as “I am sure.”

Each group contains a question, the original answer, and both rewrites. I
froze exactly 5,000 groups, including the initial 50 that I had reviewed more
generally. I used 4,000 groups to fit the probes and 1,000 held-out groups to
evaluate them. The two versions of a source always stay together. The earlier
reviewed examples stay in training.

This is a larger candidate dataset, not 5,000 human-certified pairs.
UltraChat itself contains synthetic conversations, so the source answer is
not a guarantee of factual correctness. Content-preservation review of the
expanded set is still needed.

中文提示：5,000 是来源组数，产生 10,000 个改写文本，另有 5,000 个原回答。不是 5,000 个测试样本，真正测试的是 1,000 组。

## 4 to 5.5 minutes  What the probe actually does

I keep Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct completely frozen. I give it the question and one
written response, then collect its internal states as it reads the response.
These are supplied responses, not fresh answers generated by Qwen.

For each of the 28 layers, I average the hidden states over the response
tokens. So the main analysis does not use just the question's last token or
the response's last token. A hidden state is simply the model's numerical
representation at a particular position and processing layer.

I then fit a small linear probe. It assigns a score to that representation
and learns which direction tends to separate the confident and hesitant
versions in the training pairs. Only this small probe is trained.

The fixed main comparison is layer 14 against an equal average of all 28
layer scores. The layer scores are put on comparable scales using training
data only. I also show every individual layer rather than selecting whichever
layer happens to look best on the test set.

中文提示：大模型不训练。每层取“整个回答 token 的平均状态”，然后训练一把小尺子给状态打分。

## 7.5 to 9 minutes  What this means for SDT

This study tests whether we have a candidate readout to investigate further.
It does not yet test whether pushing that score during training would improve
learning. Reading a distinction and changing it causally are different steps.

The proposed training objective is ordinary SDT loss plus a weighted internal
confidence loss. For example, we could eventually encourage a higher frozen
readout score while training on the target content. The weight would control
how much that term influences learning. That is a proposal, not a training
run I have completed.

The main risk is that such a loss might reward confident wording or some
other shortcut without improving factual recall. I would therefore judge the
training experiment using independent questions about the target facts,
paraphrased tests and retention, not just the probe score we optimize.

中文提示：SDT 的潜在帮助是更好地学会与保留内容，不是证明这些内容本来就真实，也不是让模型说话更强硬。

## 9 to 10 minutes  Next step and group discussion

My next priority is to audit a stratified sample of the expanded pairs and
test the readout on new wording or a different rewrite recipe. I especially
want to understand how much the result survives when length and obvious
certainty expressions are controlled. Correctness and evidence support can be
measured separately without changing the current paired-text labels.

If that gives us a readout worth intervening on, I would discuss a small
training pilot comparing ordinary SDT with the additional internal loss and
matched controls. The question would be whether it improves learning, rather
than whether we can increase the score.

The feedback I would most value today is whether this is the right
representation target for the intended SDT experiment, and which transfer
test would be most informative before moving to an intervention.

## 备用公式  被问方法时再展开

Response representation at layer l:

```math
\bar h_l = \frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^{T} h_{l,t},
\qquad s_l = w_l^\top z_l(\bar h_l).
```

T 只数回答的普通 token，不包括问题、padding 或结束标记。z 是只用训练集拟合的标准化。w 是训练出来的线性方向。

The paired logistic objective encourages a higher score for the confident version:

```math
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{probe}}
=\frac{1}{N}\sum_i\log\!\left(1+\exp\left[-\left(s_i^{C}-s_i^{H}\right)\right]\right)
+\text{L2 regularization}.
```

This describes the fitted probe, not a new language-model training loss. C means confident and H means hesitant. Mirrored pair differences carry equal half weights in the implementation.

Future SDT objective, not yet implemented or tested:

```math
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{total}}
=\mathcal L_{\mathrm{SDT}}+\lambda\mathcal L_{\mathrm{confidence}}.
```

## 可能被问到的问题

**为什么第 14 层？** 它是之前研究沿用的固定比较点，不是本次看完测试结果后选出的最佳层。这次同时报告全部 28 层和固定 ensemble。

**你现在取哪个 token？** 主结果是整个回答的 token 状态平均值；最后一个回答 token 只是额外对照。最初 Stage 0 的问题末尾 token 属于另一个实验。

**你证明 confidence 和 correctness 无关了吗？** 没有。我们的对照说明两者不能直接等同，并没有证明它们统计独立或存在完全正交的机制。

**有问题 question 是必要的吗？** 不是所有 confidence probing 都必须有问题。这一轮选择正常信息型问答，是为了遵循当前研究方向，并让模型在一致的聊天结构下读取回答。

**你已经改善 SDT 了吗？** 还没有。这次只训练测量用的小 probe，没有更新语言模型，也没有训练额外 loss。

**1 万组呢？** 生成与这份 5,000 组分析分开运行。新的组不会进入已经固定的训练或测试集。
